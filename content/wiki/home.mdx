# <Icon large name="pack" /> Resource Pack Wiki


## <Icon name="smile" /> Introduction

Welcome to one of the [oldest](https://www.spigotmc.org/threads/576970) resources for **server-side resource pack development**!
Here you will find useful resources and tips for developing your own,
custom and unique servers, resource packs and plugins, utilizing the most recent known mechanics.


<details>
	<summary>**What is server-side resource pack development?**</summary>

	<p>
		The vanilla Minecraft client is pretty limited in what it can do customization-wise.
		To get around those limitations we can send a resource pack to the client.
		The resource pack tells the client which models, textures and sounds to use in-game.

		Server-side plugins can then add functionality based on the previously loaded resource pack.
		This allows for modded-like content, with the functionality being handled by plugins and
		visuals handled by a resource pack.
	</p>

	Examples of what's possible:
	* Mapping custom font characters to a texture png sheet of emojis, which can then be shown anywhere
	* Having items with interchangeable models act as furniture in the world as item display entities

	**See more [real world examples](/examples)**
</details>
